Maldives Visa Information

The Maldives offers a free 30-day tourist visa on arrival for all Pakistani passport holders. No prior application or embassy visit is required.

  • Valid Pakistani Passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Confirmed Return Air Ticket
  • Hotel Voucher / Resort Confirmation
  • Proof of Sufficient Funds
  • Zero visa fee — completely free
💳

Currency & Payments

Understanding the payment landscape in the Maldives helps you budget accurately and avoid unnecessary costs during your stay.

  • Local currency is Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR)
  • US Dollars (USD) are widely accepted at all resorts
  • All major credit cards accepted at resorts
  • Carry small USD bills for tips and local shopping
  • Exchange PKR to USD before departure for best rates
